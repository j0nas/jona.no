A Raspberry Pi with a 2.7-inch e-paper screen in a case I printed. Four buttons switch between bus departures, the weather with a three-day forecast, a poem of the day and the battery levels of our IKEA smart-home devices. The test for what goes on it is whether it changes what I do in the next few hours. It refreshes the screen sparingly and comes back by itself after a power cut.
